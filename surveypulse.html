The alert comes first

Be alerted. Notice the assignment. Respond.

SurveyPulse is primarily an audible alert system—not merely a register of allocation messages. Its purpose is to make sure a new assignment does not go unnoticed.

🚨

Immediate audible alarm

When a supported Insurers' allocation SMS arrives, SurveyPulse sounds an alarm to draw your attention to the new survey assignment. You do not have to keep checking your messages.

🔔

Repeat alarm until acknowledged

If you have not acknowledged the new assignment, SurveyPulse repeats the alarm at your configured interval. This reminder is the key safeguard against an allocation going unnoticed.

📩

Automatic SMS monitoring

SurveyPulse monitors supported Insurers' allocation SMS messages and detects incoming survey job offers so that the alert can be raised promptly.

One-tap acknowledgement

Mark an allocation as acknowledged when you have attended to it. Acknowledged allocations are excluded from pending reminders.
